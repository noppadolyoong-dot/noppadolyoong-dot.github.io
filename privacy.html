AUTOPOST Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

AUTOPOST is a private tool that runs on its owner's computer and posts scheduled content to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

What data AUTOPOST accesses

  • YouTube: when the owner connects a channel, AUTOPOST asks Google for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload), to read the channel's name and ID (youtube.readonly), and to manage its videos (youtube.force-ssl). It uses these only to upload the videos the owner has queued, set their publish time and cover image, show which channel is connected, and delete a video when the owner deletes that post in AUTOPOST.
  • TikTok: when the owner connects an account, AUTOPOST asks TikTok for permission to read the account's basic profile (user.info.basic, to show which account is connected) and to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload). The owner adds the caption and publishes each draft in the TikTok app; AUTOPOST never publishes to TikTok by itself.
  • Google Sheets and Google Drive: the post queue and the media files the owner has shared with the tool.
  • Facebook and Instagram: the Pages and accounts the owner has set up, in order to publish the owner's posts, and to delete a post when the owner deletes it in AUTOPOST.

How data is stored

Access tokens and settings are stored only in files on the owner's computer. AUTOPOST has no server and keeps no database elsewhere. It does not collect analytics.

Sharing

AUTOPOST does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data is sent only to the platform being posted to (Google/YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) as needed to publish the owner's content.

Google API Services

AUTOPOST's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect YouTube at any time in AUTOPOST, or revoke its access from their Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further uploads. TikTok access can be removed in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
