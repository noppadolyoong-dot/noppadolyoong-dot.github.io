AUTOPOST

A private, self-hosted tool for scheduling posts to the owner's own social media accounts.

AUTOPOST runs on its owner's computer. It reads a queue of posts from the owner's Google Sheet and schedules each one on the platforms chosen for it — Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

For YouTube, the owner connects their own channel once with Google sign-in. AUTOPOST then uploads the videos the owner has queued and sets the time they go public. It does not read, change or delete anything else on the channel.

For TikTok, the owner connects their own account once. At each post's scheduled time AUTOPOST uploads the video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft; the owner then finishes and publishes it in the TikTok app.

AUTOPOST is not a public service. It has no user accounts and is used only by its owner.
